## Ways of working

- I go looking for hard problems. An unfamiliar domain or a tool I have never used is the interesting part, not the obstacle.
- I learn by building. The fastest way to understand something is to ship a small working version of it.
- I break things on purpose to find out how they actually work, then rebuild them with that knowledge.
- I keep changing fields when there is something new to learn: brewery floor, growth teams, regulated software, AI products.
