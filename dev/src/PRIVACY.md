# PRIVACY.md

> Short version: this site is a pile of Markdown files. It does not want your data.

## Who is responsible

The controller under the GDPR is Felipe Meloni, Germany. Email: mail@lfpmeloni.dev.

## What this site collects

Nothing personal that you type in. There is no form, no login, no comment box, and no account. Anonymous visit statistics are described under Analytics, and hosting logs under Hosting.

## Cookies

None. The site sets no cookies, analytics included, and uses no browser storage for tracking. The only thing it remembers is your day/night choice, kept in your own browser's local storage because you asked for it, and never sent anywhere.

## Analytics

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to count visits and measure page speed. They record the page viewed, the referring site (for example a search engine or LinkedIn), the country, and the browser, operating system and device type, plus loading-time measurements. They set no cookies. A visitor is recognised only by an anonymous hash of the request that resets every day, so visits can't be followed across days or across other websites, and no profile is built.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my legitimate interest in knowing which pages are read and keeping them fast.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel, which processes standard request logs (IP address, user agent, requested path, time) to serve and protect the site. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in delivering the site securely.

Vercel Inc. acts as my processor under its Data Processing Addendum. It is based in the US; transfers there rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the Standard Contractual Clauses included in that addendum.

## Email

If you write to mail@lfpmeloni.dev, that email is read and kept only to reply to you. It is not added to any list and not shared. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR, answering what you asked.

## Third parties

Outbound links (GitHub, LinkedIn) are plain links. Clicking one may be counted as an anonymous event, without anything that identifies you. Opening them is between you and them.

## Your rights

You have the right to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). Write to mail@lfpmeloni.dev.

You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one responsible here: the Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg (LfDI BW).
